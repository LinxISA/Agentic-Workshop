# PyCircuit section: authoring background

The [main outline](pycircuit-outline.md) is the narrative authority; the [section checklist](slide-authoring-checklist.md) defines later authoring tasks. No slide count or duration is fixed.

Keep the planned completed-flow narrative separate from claims about current implementation. The method connects NDF contracts, architect/Agent refinement, typed hardware descriptions, compiler feedback and independent evidence. Requirement-to-code traceability can be many-to-many. Governance remains an open research question.

Later authors should choose a disclosed, reproducible source snapshot and accurately referenced examples. Do not publish local-only source maps, private implementation excerpts or personal paths. Do not infer behavioral proof from shared IR, structural checks or test-file presence.

See [reference entry guidance](../sources/pycircuit-reference-entries.md), [related work](../sources/related-work.md) and [Q&A](pycircuit-ndf-discussion.md).
