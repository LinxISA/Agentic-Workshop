# Superscalar NPU: provenance and evidence boundaries

The outline draws on a supplied author article, 《辩经系列之六：超标量或者静态排流水》. The original text, local file provenance and private implementation mappings are retained locally and excluded from this repository.

The article advocates architectural mechanisms; it does not establish universal superiority or a complete-core implementation. Numerical examples and performance/utilization/area estimates lack sufficient baseline and measurement provenance for public comparison. Do not present them as measured results. Later authors must use approved evidence or label illustrative assumptions explicitly.

The [outline](../talks/superscalar-npu/outline.md) and [eight-problem summary](../talks/superscalar-npu/problem-summary.md) record discussion scope, not measured outcomes. Late allocation, safe release, backpressure and forward progress remain design obligations. No quantitative reproduction is supplied.
